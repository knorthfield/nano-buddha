# Nano Buddha privacy policy

Nano Buddha does not collect, send or share any of your data.

- Your sits and settings stay on your devices.
- Nano Buddha keeps your devices the same through your own iCloud account (iPhone, iPad, Apple
  Watch, Apple TV and Mac) and directly between your iPhone and Apple Watch. The developer
  cannot see this data.
- If you give permission, Nano Buddha saves each sit to Apple Health as Mindful Minutes. It does
  not read any data from Apple Health.
- Nano Buddha has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

You can remove Apple Health access at any time in the Health app or in Settings.
